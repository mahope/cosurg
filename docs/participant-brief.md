"Every clinical conversation contains the answers. Most of them go unheard."

Clinicians speak with patients every day, gathering history, explaining diagnoses, catching the subtle signal that changes a decision. But most of that conversation disappears. What gets documented is a fraction of what was said. What gets acted on is a fraction of what was documented.

The Corti platform gives you real-time and post-encounter access to that conversation. Your challenge: build something that makes it matter.

### The Challenge

Build something that makes clinical conversations more useful: for the clinician, the patient, or the people coordinating care around them.

That might look like:

Something that surfaces a critical signal in the moment, before the encounter ends

Something that automates a task after the visit that currently eats a clinician's time

Something that finds a pattern across thousands of encounters that no one thought to look for

Something that connects conversation data to other systems to close a gap in the care journey

#### Clinical Problem Examples

Taking an accurate patient medical history can be challenging if the content is complex, the patient explains poorly or the clinician is tired, busy or interrupted frequently.

Reading the patient record can be time-consuming and difficult, which risks an incomplete or inaccurate clinician understanding.

Under time pressure, documentation is often done through copy-and-paste from previous notes, which can carry forward outdated or inaccurate information into the current record.

Rare conditions or rare combinations are often not well-known by medical professionals, leading to a risk of delay in diagnosis or wrong treatment.

Patients do not always know what is clinically relevant, so they may spend their limited time with the clinician explaining things that do not change the outcome, while things that do matter go unmentioned.

Documentation burden takes time away from direct patient care and sometimes cannot be finished during the day.

### Technical Requirements

#### Presentation Requirements:

Each team member must introduce themselves and state their team name and number.

No more than 5 minutes with 2 minutes of questions.

The full audio transcription can be pre-completed, but you must show that the speech to text is functional.

You cannot vote for your own project.

#### Restrictions/Rules:

Do not use prewritten code from anything you have built. (Open source and public packages are fine)

No more than 4 per team.

Do not use any AI API except Corti. However, you may use Text to Speech models, OCR models, and MCP connections connected by Corti’s agentic framework.

In your submission repo, please include a file with what other products/datasets were used.
