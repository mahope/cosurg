## 4. Answers to the questions you will get (2 minutes)

**"How does it know whether I am asking a question or answering one?"**
> "In three layers, ordered by what the mistake costs. A rule in the browser decides
> only what it is certain of — instantly, with no network, and it can tell you why:
> 'it begins with how'. What it is not certain of goes to Corti's interpreter. And only
> when *that* is in doubt do we ask a Corti agent whether it was a question after all.
> If it is genuinely ambiguous, we stop and ask the doctor. We never pick the likelier
> reading, because an answer read as a question costs a repetition — and a question
> read as an answer costs a decision."

**"How do you know the interpretation is right?"**
> "Every node has a closed set of allowed answers. The agent maps onto one of them or
> flags it as unclear and asks again. It is never allowed to invent a value. And the
> recommendation itself is not interpreted at all — it is the tree."

**"What if the model is wrong?"**
> "Then it asks again. The failure mode is a repeated question, not a wrong
> recommendation. The recommendation is deterministic — the same path always gives the
> same answer."

**"Isn't this just a chatbot with a wrapper?"**
> "The difference between a chatbot and clinical decision support is not how well it
> writes. It is where the answer comes from. The recommendation comes from a tree
> clinicians wrote. The codes come from Corti's coding API instead of being invented.
> The lookups are verbatim from named sources — and when our sources say nothing, the
> app says so instead of filling the silence."

**"Who wrote the clinical content?"**
> "We did. The burn tree is derived from brandsaar.dk, the Danish Burn Association's
> guidelines, written by Rami. The dressing guide is from Rigshospitalet's burn unit,
> section 6052 — the national referral centre for burns — brought in by Magnus. Every
> tree carries its sources and its named authors in the file itself."

**"Isn't this just another scribe?"**
> "A scribe writes down what happened. This changes what happens — it asks the
> question the doctor didn't get to, and it answers the one he didn't have time to
> look up."

**"What about background noise in the operating theatre?"**
> "Known limitation. The commands are matched conservatively: five words maximum,
> exact phrases only. A wake word is on the roadmap."

## 5. IF IT GOES WRONG

Stay calm. There is a way forward in every case. **Never just stand there and wait.**

### A. The microphone does not work — a red panel says "Permission denied" or similar

1. Press the **padlock** to the left of the address at the top of the browser.
2. Set **Microphone** to **Allow**.
3. Press **F5** to reload the page. Start that part over.

If it still does not work: **type instead.** Every line in the script goes into the
same field, whether it is spoken or typed. Magnus types the line and presses Enter.
Tell the judges: *"I'll type it — same path through the app."* This is not a failure,
it is the other way in.

On the opening screen there are also three ready-made examples under the field.
Clicking one **fills the field** with it — use the patient example if the microphone
is dead and you want the intake to look effortless.

### B. The intake does not open the pathway — the app asks which one you meant

That is the app refusing to guess, and it is worth saying out loud:
*"It hit two pathways, so it asks instead of choosing."*
Then press **Burns — acute assessment** in the row of buttons and carry on.

### C. The lookup says our sources do not cover the topic

**Do not treat this as a failure — it is the strongest sentence in the demo.** Say:

> "And there it is: our sources do not cover that, so it says so. It does not fill the
> silence with something that sounds right."

Then press **Back to the question** and carry on with Part A. If you have time and want
an answer on screen, press **Ask the literature instead** — but be aware it can take up
to a minute, so only do it if you are ahead of schedule.

If you would rather retry with a topic that is certain to be covered, ask instead:
**"How do I treat a chemical burn?"**

### D. The lookup is slow — more than 20 seconds

Keep narrating; there is a full paragraph for it in Part A. If it passes 30 seconds,
press **Back to the question**, say *"I'll show you the answer afterwards"*, and carry
on with the pathway. The pathway is untouched — that is exactly the point you were
making.

### E. It says "Interpreting…" and nothing more happens

The network is gone. **The app will not recover on its own — do not wait.**

1. Press the **answer button** on screen instead (e.g. `Scald`). It works without a
   network.
2. If that does not help: press **F5**. The page restarts at the opening field.
3. If the network is completely gone: skip to point J.

### F. The app does not understand the answer and asks again

Completely normal, and in fact the point. Tell the judges:
*"There — it wasn't sure, so it asked again instead of guessing."*
Then say just **one word**: `"Scald."`, `"No."`, `"Yes."`. Not a whole sentence.

### G. There is no sound

Turn the volume up. Check that audio is not routed to another output (headphones,
projector). If the voice suddenly sounds more robotic than yesterday: that is the
built-in browser voice stepping in because the good voice could not be reached.
**Carry on — everything works.** Do not mention it.

### H. Handsfree mode: you say "next" and nothing happens

Magnus presses the **space bar**. It does exactly the same as "next".
Right arrow = next. Left arrow = back. **R** = repeat.
Tell the judges: *"My colleague is not scrubbed — so he can press."*

### I. The red panel will not go away

Say **"OK."** Or press the **OK** button. Or press space.

### J. The page is blank, or nothing works

1. **F5.**
2. If that fails: close the browser, open it again, go to the address.
3. If it still fails: **tell the story instead of showing it.** Say: *"Our connection
   is down, so let me tell you what you would have seen — and I am happy to show it to
   you right after."* Then walk through the clinical problem and how CoSurg answers it,
   using the printed script. A judge who understands the idea will remember it; a demo
   nobody could follow is worth less than a clear explanation.

### K. The venue wifi is dead

Share internet from a phone (personal hotspot) and connect the computer to it.
If that fails: point J.

## 7. Nødplan og huskeseddel — på dansk

*(Afsnit 5 og 6 på dansk. Det er dem I læser under pres.)*

### Hvis det går galt

Ro på. Der er en vej videre i alle tilfældene. **Bliv aldrig stående og vent.**

**A. Mikrofonen virker ikke — der står "Permission denied" eller lignende i et rødt felt**
1. Tryk på **hængelåsen** til venstre for adressen øverst i browseren.
2. Sæt **Mikrofon** til **Tillad**.
3. Tryk **F5** for at genindlæse siden. Start forfra på delen.

Virker det stadig ikke: **skriv i stedet.** Alle replikker i manuskriptet går ned i
samme felt, uanset om de siges eller skrives. Magnus skriver linjen og trykker Enter.
Sig til dommerne: *"I'll type it — same path through the app."* Det er ikke en fejl,
det er den anden vej ind.

På forsiden ligger der desuden tre færdige eksempler under feltet. Et klik **udfylder
feltet** med eksemplet — brug patient-eksemplet hvis mikrofonen er død og indgangen
skal se ubesværet ud.

**B. Indgangen åbner ikke forløbet — appen spørger hvilket I mente**
Det er appen der nægter at gætte, og det er værd at sige højt:
*"It hit two pathways, so it asks instead of choosing."*
Tryk så på **Burns — acute assessment** i knapperækken og kør videre.

**C. Opslaget siger at vores kilder ikke dækker emnet**
**Behandl det ikke som en fejl — det er demoens stærkeste sætning.** Sig:
*"And there it is: our sources do not cover that, so it says so. It does not fill the
silence with something that sounds right."*
Tryk derefter **Back to the question** og fortsæt Del A. Har I tid og vil have et svar
på skærmen, så tryk **Ask the literature instead** — men vær klar over at det kan tage
op mod et minut, så gør det kun hvis I er foran.
Vil I hellere prøve igen med et emne der med sikkerhed er dækket, så spørg i stedet:
**"How do I treat a chemical burn?"**

**D. Opslaget er langsomt — mere end 20 sekunder**
Bliv ved med at fortælle; der er et helt afsnit til det i Del A. Går der mere end 30
sekunder: tryk **Back to the question**, sig *"I'll show you the answer afterwards"*,
og kør videre i forløbet. Forløbet står uændret — og det var netop pointen.

**E. Der står "Interpreting…" og der sker ikke mere**
Nettet er væk. **Appen kommer ikke videre af sig selv — vent ikke.**
1. Tryk i stedet på **svarknappen** på skærmen (fx `Scald`). Den virker uden net.
2. Hjælper det ikke: tryk **F5**. Siden starter forfra på indgangsfeltet.
3. Er nettet helt væk: spring til punkt J.

**F. Appen forstår ikke svaret og spørger igen**
Helt normalt og faktisk en pointe. Sig til dommerne:
*"There — it wasn't sure, so it asked again instead of guessing."*
Sig så bare **ét ord**: `"Scald."`, `"No."`, `"Yes."`. Ikke en hel sætning.

**G. Der kommer ingen lyd**
Skru op. Tjek at lyden ikke er sat til et andet output (hovedtelefoner, projektor).
Lyder stemmen pludselig mere robotagtig end i går: det er den indbyggede
browserstemme der er trådt til fordi den gode stemme ikke kunne nås. **Fortsæt —
alt virker.** Nævn det ikke.

**H. Håndfri tilstand: I siger "next", men der sker ingenting**
Magnus trykker på **mellemrumstasten**. Det gør præcis det samme som "next".
Højrepil = næste. Venstrepil = tilbage. **R** = gentag.
Sig til dommerne: *"My colleague is not scrubbed — so he can press."*

**I. Det røde felt vil ikke forsvinde**
Sig **"OK."** Eller tryk på **OK**-knappen. Eller tryk mellemrum.

**J. Siden er hvid, eller intet virker**
1. **F5.**
2. Virker det ikke: luk browseren, åbn den igen, gå til adressen.
3. Virker det stadig ikke: **fortæl det i stedet for at vise det.** Sig: *"Our
   connection is down, so let me tell you what you would have seen — and I am
   happy to show it to you right after."* Gennemgå så det kliniske problem og
   hvordan CoSurg løser det, ud fra det trykte manuskript. En dommer der forstår
   idéen husker den; en demo ingen kunne følge er mindre værd end en klar
   forklaring.

**K. Wifi'et på stedet er dødt**
Del internet fra en telefon (personligt hotspot) og forbind computeren til det.
Virker det ikke: punkt J.
